WorkOS Migration — Launch Plan

Draft for review · August 18, 2026 · Owner: Jessica Manner

Launch blocker — ENDER-37178: WorkOS blocks tenant/contact email edits and prevents onboarding-link testing. In-app email-edit is blocked and unfixed (status: In Progress). Must be resolved before the mid-Sept release.
Overview

We're migrating login and account-info management to WorkOS AuthKit. Applicants, residents, and vendors will be able to sign up and log in with a Google or Microsoft account (Social Login) or a one-time emailed code (Magic Auth) instead of an Ender password. Internally, Ender/EG employees log in via their company identity provider (Enterprise SSO) — Ender via Google, EG via their Microsoft/Entra ID account. Once an account is tied to one of these, profile edits (starting with email) are managed through that identity provider rather than in-product.

Rollback Plan

Launch is gated behind a feature flag — if something's catastrophic, we flip it off and public flows revert to the legacy login path. Flow 7 above suggests links issued before a rollback still resolve safely. Rollback triggers on any one of the following:

  • Any one of the 9 core flows above is confirmed broken for any user — no threshold needed, auth is binary.
  • Login/onboarding failure rate exceeds ~5–10% over a sustained 15-minute window.
  • An abnormal spike in login-related support tickets relative to traffic.

Exact mechanics (flag scope, who can flip it, what happens to accounts already created in WorkOS, monitoring) still TBD — Jess + Eng to align separately.
